For Individuals, Self Advocates & Families

Your Self Direction.
Your Choices. Your Next Steps.

Whether you are a Self Advocate managing your own Self Direction, an individual beginning to explore your options, or a family member supporting someone you care about, SelfDirectConnect is designed to help you understand the process, find useful resources, and make informed decisions.

You should be able to see yourself in the information you use. This space is designed for the people directing their own lives and for the families and supporters walking alongside them.

This Page Is For You

If you are a Self Advocate, this is your Self Direction journey. The goal is to help you better understand your options, ask informed questions, and make choices that reflect what matters to you.

If you are an individual exploring Self Direction, you do not need to already know all of the terminology or how every part of the process works. You can begin by learning one step at a time.

If you are a parent, family member, or supporter, SelfDirectConnect can help you find clearer information and prepare for conversations while keeping the individual’s voice, preferences, and goals at the center.

Questions You May Be Asking

SelfDirectConnect is being built around the real questions individuals and families face when trying to understand and navigate Self Direction.

Where do I start?

Begin with the basics. Learn what Self Direction is, who may be involved, and what choices you may have before trying to solve everything at once.

What does a Support Broker do?

Learn about the Support Broker role, the kind of support a broker may provide, and questions you can consider when deciding whether someone may be a good fit.

How do I know what support I need?

Your goals, communication preferences, current supports, and the areas where you want more help can all shape what professional support may be useful.

How do I choose someone to work with?

Look beyond a name on a list. Consider experience, communication, availability, approach, location or service area, and whether the professional relationship feels right for you.

Choosing a Support Broker Is a Personal Decision

SelfDirectConnect can help make professional information easier to find, but the final decision should remain with the individual and the people they choose to involve.

✓
Think about what matters most to you before beginning your search.
✓
Ask questions about experience, communication style, availability, and approach.
✓
Take time to decide whether the person understands your goals and respects your choices.
✓
Confirm official requirements and service details through the appropriate OPWDD and support channels.
SelfDirectConnect does not choose a Support Broker for you, guarantee availability or services, or endorse a professional simply because they appear on the platform.
