About SelfDirectConnect

Our Mission. Our Purpose.
Your Independence.

SelfDirectConnect was created to make Self Direction easier to understand, easier to navigate, and easier to connect within. We bring education, practical resources, and professional connections together so individuals and families can move forward with greater clarity and confidence.

SelfDirectConnect is designed to support informed choice while keeping individuals with disabilities at the center of decisions about their own lives.
Our Story

Why SelfDirectConnect Exists

Self Direction can offer meaningful choice and control, but navigating it can feel fragmented. Individuals, Self Advocates, and families may have to piece together information from different places while trying to understand roles, processes, and professional options.

SelfDirectConnect was created to organize that experience. Our goal is to provide one accessible place where people can learn, prepare, discover resources, and connect with professionals who participate in the Self Direction community.

We are building for clarity, connection, and informed choice. SelfDirectConnect is not designed to replace OPWDD, Care Managers, Support Brokers, Fiscal Intermediaries, or other qualified professionals.

What Guides Our Work

SelfDirectConnect is being built around values that support dignity, access, independence, and responsible professional connection.

01

Accessible Information

We aim to make important information easier to understand, navigate, enlarge, and use with assistive technology.

02

Informed Choice

People should have clearer information and stronger connections so they can make decisions that reflect their own goals and preferences.

03

Responsible Connection

Professional visibility should be built around accurate information, clear participation standards, and transparency about what the platform can and cannot do.

Who We Serve

SelfDirectConnect is designed for the people making Self Direction decisions and the professionals and supporters who help them move forward.

Self Advocates & Individuals

People directing their own lives who want clearer information, practical resources, and professional connections.

Families & Supporters

Parents, relatives, and trusted supporters helping someone understand options and prepare for next steps.

Support Brokers

Professionals who want to clearly present their experience, services, and availability to the Self Direction community.

Care Managers & Partners

Referral professionals and community partners looking for organized information and useful connections.

Independent Platform. Clear Boundaries.

SelfDirectConnect is designed to make information and professional connections easier to find without presenting itself as an official government service or making decisions for users.

✓
SelfDirectConnect is not an OPWDD agency or official government platform.
✓
We do not determine eligibility, approve services, authorize payments, or replace individualized planning.
✓
Professional listings do not automatically mean endorsement or guarantee a successful service relationship.
✓
Official program requirements should continue to be confirmed through appropriate OPWDD and support channels.
SelfDirectConnect is an independent information and professional connection platform. It does not replace official program guidance, professional advice, or individualized service planning.
