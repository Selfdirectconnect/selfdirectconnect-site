Our Story

Why SelfDirectConnect exists

Self Direction can offer meaningful choice and control, but the experience of navigating it is often fragmented. Individuals and families may have to piece together information from different sources while trying to understand roles, processes, and professional options.

SelfDirectConnect was created to organize that experience. Our goal is to provide one accessible place where people can learn, prepare, discover resources, and connect with professionals who participate in the Self Direction ecosystem.

We are building infrastructure for clarity.
SelfDirectConnect is designed to support informed choice, not replace the role of OPWDD, care managers, Support Brokers, fiscal intermediaries, or other qualified professionals.
