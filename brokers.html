A Better Way to Be Seen and Understood

Support Brokers often rely on word of mouth, informal referral networks, and scattered contact lists to connect with individuals and families. That can make it difficult for people to understand a broker's experience, service approach, and availability before reaching out.

SelfDirectConnect is designed to create a more organized professional presence. Participating brokers can present clear information about what they do while individuals and families gain a better way to explore potential professional connections.

What the Platform Is Designed to Offer Brokers

Building Trust ResponsiblySelfDirectConnect is beginning with detailed professional profiles and clear Participation Standards. As the platform grows, we plan to introduce a structured feedback and rating system designed to help individuals and families learn from the experiences of others when considering Support Brokers. Feedback features will be introduced with clear participation, moderation, and dispute standards. Review the full Participation Standards.

For Brokers Who Are Also Parents or Family Members

Being a parent, guardian, relative, or household member does not automatically prevent you from participating on SelfDirectConnect or from serving other eligible clients.

SelfDirectConnect may not be used to seek, advertise, arrange, or receive payment for Support Broker services in a family or household relationship where current OPWDD rules prohibit that payment.

Each broker is responsible for following current OPWDD requirements. SelfDirectConnect does not determine payment eligibility or authorize payment for brokerage services. Attempts to use the platform to circumvent applicable payment restrictions may result in suspension or removal.
