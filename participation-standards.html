Participation Standards

SelfDirectConnect is being built to strengthen clarity, accountability, and coordination within the self direction ecosystem.

Participation on this platform follows defined standards designed to protect individuals, families, professionals, and the integrity of the system.

1. Opt In Participation

No professional profile will be published without explicit consent. Participation is voluntary and may be withdrawn at any time, subject to active engagement policies.

2. Verification and Accuracy

Participants are responsible for ensuring that all submitted information is accurate and current.

  • Professional credentials
  • Service scope
  • Areas of expertise
  • Organizational affiliation

Misrepresentation may result in profile suspension or removal.

3. Structured Profile Framework

Profiles follow a standardized format to ensure clarity and fairness. They are structured professional representations, not endorsements by SelfDirectConnect.

4. Family Relationship and Payment Compliance

Some Support Brokers are also parents, guardians, relatives, or household members of people using Self Direction. Those relationships do not automatically prevent a professional from participating on SelfDirectConnect or from serving other eligible clients.

However, participants may not use SelfDirectConnect to seek, advertise, arrange, or receive payment for Support Broker services in any family or household relationship where current OPWDD rules prohibit that payment.

Each participating broker is responsible for understanding and following current OPWDD requirements that apply to their own circumstances. SelfDirectConnect does not determine payment eligibility and does not authorize, approve, or guarantee payment for brokerage services.

Attempting to use the platform to circumvent applicable OPWDD payment restrictions may result in profile suspension or removal.

5. Structured Feedback Framework (Future Phase)

A structured feedback and rating system is planned for a future phase. When implemented, it will:

  • Follow defined submission guidelines
  • Prohibit defamatory or unverified claims
  • Include a dispute and review process
  • Allow contextual response where appropriate

SelfDirectConnect reserves the right to moderate, delay, or decline publication of submissions.

6. Professional Conduct Expectations

Participants agree to:

  • Maintain respectful communication
  • Avoid misleading service representations
  • Engage in good faith
  • Follow applicable program requirements and platform participation rules

7. Platform Integrity

SelfDirectConnect may update participation standards as the platform evolves. The platform reserves the right to suspend participation, remove inaccurate content, and enforce moderation policies.

Our Vision

Self Direction continues to grow. As it grows, structure becomes essential.

Participation on SelfDirectConnect is not simply about visibility. It is about contributing to a more organized, accountable, and coordinated ecosystem.
