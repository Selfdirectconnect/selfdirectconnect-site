How SelfDirectConnect Works

Self Direction Can Feel Complicated.
Finding Your Next Step Shouldn’t.

SelfDirectConnect brings information, practical resources, and professional connections together to help you better understand your options and move forward with greater confidence.

One accessible place to learn, explore resources, build professional connections, and make more informed decisions about your next steps.

A Clearer Way to Navigate Self Direction

Self Direction involves important choices, different professional roles, and information that can sometimes feel scattered across multiple places.

SelfDirectConnect is designed to organize useful information and professional connections into a clearer experience. Instead of trying to figure out where to begin, visitors can learn about their options, explore practical resources, and connect with Support Brokers through one growing platform.

From Questions to Clearer Next Steps

The platform is built around a simple journey that helps you move forward without adding another complicated process.

01

Learn

Understand Self Direction, professional roles, and the options available to you through clear, plain language information.

02

Explore

Use practical resources to answer common questions and better understand what to consider before making decisions.

03

Connect

Discover Support Brokers and professional connections that may support your Self Direction goals.

04

Move Forward

Use what you have learned to make more informed choices about the next steps that make sense for you.

What SelfDirectConnect Brings Together

The goal is not to replace the Self Direction process. It is to make useful information and professional connections easier to find and understand.

Clear Information

Plain language information designed to help individuals and families better understand Self Direction and the roles involved.

Practical Resources

Useful guidance built around real questions, decisions, and common points of confusion.

Support Broker Connections

A growing professional connection platform where individuals and families can learn more about participating Support Brokers.

A More Organized Experience

A central place designed to reduce the need to search across scattered sources when looking for information and professional support.

Information and Connection, Not Decisions for You

SelfDirectConnect is designed to support informed choice. The platform provides information and opportunities to connect while keeping decisions where they belong: with individuals, families, and their support teams.
